# Demo Flow

1. Open the public feed and show category/status filters.
2. Submit a claim containing BREAKING / SHOCKING / SHARE BEFORE DELETED, lots of uppercase text, and no source URL.
3. Open the claim detail and show the flags and High Risk badge.
4. Open Review, select a verdict, and add a short evidence note.
5. Return to the feed and filter to show the updated status.
6. Show Delete Review and Delete Claim behavior.
